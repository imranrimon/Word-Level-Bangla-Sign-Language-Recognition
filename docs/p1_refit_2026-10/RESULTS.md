## Interpretation (descriptive; the verdict above is the binding pre-registered one)

**Both decision criteria fail, so the verdict is REFRAME.**

* **D1 fails:** P(b<0) = 0.74. The slope's 95% CrI [−14.8, +8.6] includes 0.
* **D2 fails:** leave-one-language-out sign accuracy is 2/7.
* **No pre-registered sensitivity analysis comes close to either threshold.** The best is S3 (no language term), at P(b<0) = 0.81.
* **The replication-only fit is flat:** P(b<0) = 0.49, b = +0.2 [−12.6, +13.2]. It drops pseed 0, the seed that generated the hypothesis.

**The P1 law is a property of pretraining seed 0.**

* pseed 0 is the only seed with a strong negative slope: R²(7) 0.89, slope −24.6.
* Across the other six seeds:
  * R²(7) ranges from 0.003 to 0.40.
  * The slope ranges from −12.3 to +17.3.
  * 3 of the 6 slopes are positive.
* Pooled over 7 seeds, P1's own estimator gives:
  * calib-5 R² 0.001 (P1: 0.997)
  * all-7 R² 0.08 (P1: 0.887)
  * Spearman ρ(7) −0.07

**What does replicate is weak, language-specific, and not ordered by distance.**

* **LIBRAS:** alignment hurts. Pooled G = −2.14, CI [−4.54, +0.21], P(G>0) = 0.04. G is negative at 6 of 7 pseeds.
* **BdSL:** alignment helps. Pooled G = +2.26, CI [−0.47, +4.91], P(G>0) = 0.95.
* **Distance ordering:** these two languages sit at d = 0.315 and d = 0.143, but the far targets do not backfire on average:
  * AUTSL +0.01
  * KArSL +1.53
  * LSFB −0.48
* **TEST:** every pooled TEST G has a CI that spans 0: BdSL +0.96, LSA64 −1.52, AUTSL −0.56.

**Variance structure (posterior medians).**

* Shared pretraining-seed effect: σ_u ≈ 1.0 pp.
* Language × pretraining-seed interaction: σ_w ≈ 2.7 pp.
* Fine-tune noise per cell: ≈ 2.6 pp.
* Language lack of fit: σ_v ≈ 1.0 pp.
* So 51% of the single-run variance in G comes from the pretraining run (ICC_pseed 0.51, CrI [0.24, 0.73]). Most of that is language-specific rather than shared (ICC_shared 0.07).
* Implication: a single pretraining run gives each language an independent draw of about ±2.7 pp. That is larger than every pooled per-language effect except BdSL's and LIBRAS's.

**BdSL headline (`tab:sig` / `tab:test`).**

* With xling over 7 pseeds and mono over 3, xling − mono = **+1.98 VAL**, CI [−1.46, +5.61], P(≤0) = 0.15.
* On TEST it is **+0.60**, CI [−2.00, +3.61].
* The matched pseed 0–2 contrast reproduces item 5 (+0.78 [−3.20, +4.97]).
* P1's seed-0 figures were +4.2 VAL and +3.9 TEST.
* Mono − scratch is +8.24 VAL, CI [+4.61, +11.65]. This SSL benefit is the robust BdSL effect. On TEST it is +1.51, CI [−1.22, +3.65].

**`tab:transfer` / `tab:robust` against pooled G.**

* Every motion-distance variant falls to R² ≤ 0.03. P1 reported 0.62–0.997.
* The naive controls and LogME now fit the 5 calibration gains best:
  * #classes 0.82
  * LogME 0.74
  * train size 0.70
* These are 5-point fits to near-zero gains, so they mean nothing either way. The defensible statement is: no estimator predicts pooled G.
* Caveat: these estimator distances share the source-mismatch flaw described above.

## Consistency check against the checkpoint-predictor study's numbers (2026-10-22)

**Matches**

* **Spearman correlations.** My per-pseed Spearman(d, G) values are −0.93 / +0.71 / +0.18 / +0.46 / +0.29 / −0.18 / −0.32. Over all 49 units, Spearman(d, G) = −0.063. The magnitudes are identical to the checkpoint-predictor study's values (+0.93 / −0.71 / −0.18 / −0.46 / −0.29 / +0.18 / +0.32; 49-unit +0.06). Every sign is flipped, so that study evidently correlated d with −G (the "law direction"). The underlying data agree.
* **Mean SE².** Mine is 6.616 pp², the same as theirs (6.62).

**Discrepancy: variance split.**

* Theirs: language 2.20, pretraining seed 3.46, residual 8.15 pp².
* Mine, cell-level REML with intercept only: language 2.01, pseed 0.26, residual (w+ε) 11.65.
  * Adding d as a fixed effect gives language 0.00, pseed 1.84, residual 11.59.
* Mine, two-way ANOVA method of moments: language 0.65, pseed 1.91, residual MS 10.87.
* The language component roughly agrees (≈2 pp² without d). The pseed main-effect variance is weakly identified with 7 pseeds: it moves between 0.3 and 1.9 depending on the estimator and the fixed effects. Theirs (3.46) is probably a run-level fit with a different residual definition.
* The qualitative conclusion is the same under every estimator: the residual (language × pseed + fine-tune noise) dominates, and the main language effect, the only part a distance could explain, is the smallest component.
